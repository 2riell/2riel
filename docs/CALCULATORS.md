# Financial calculators — 0.3.0

Calculators work offline with your own inputs. Initial amounts, rates, debt ratios and withholding percentages are **editable examples**, not current offers, recommendations or tax determinations. All amounts are PHP. They never read/write the ledger, connect to a bank, fetch rates or execute a transaction. Inputs exist only in component memory and reset when leaving Calculators. Schedule exports are plaintext; the privacy toggle disables export and hides monetary results/inputs and charts.

Calculations use decimal.js at 40-digit precision. Money inputs allow at most two decimals and are converted to integer cents; rates allow at most six decimals. Repayment balances and cash flows are integer cents. Loan/card interest is rounded half up monthly, final payments are capped to the amount owed, and every row reconciles payment = interest + principal. Zero rates are handled explicitly. Excessive terms, unsafe amounts, invalid inputs and payments that do not cover interest show an error instead of an invented result. Maximum term is 1,200 months; emergency coverage is 1–24 months.

| Tool               | Calculation and conventions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Loan & financing   | Principal = price − down payment. For nominal annual rate `a`, monthly rate `r=a/1200`; base monthly payment `P*r/(1-(1+r)^(-n))`, or `P/n` when `r=0`, rounded half up to cents. Fixed reducing balance; month-end payments. The last payment clears cent-rounding residuals. Extra monthly payments reduce principal; interest/time savings compare the same loan without extras. Total cash includes repayments, upfront down payment and fees paid separately. Fees are not financed and no EIR is calculated. |
| Affordability      | Monthly payment budget = income × chosen debt-payment share − existing monthly debt, floored at zero. Maximum principal is the present value of that payment stream, rounded down to cents. This is a planning cap, not an approval or recommendation; fees and down payment are separate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Credit-card payoff | Interest = opening monthly balance × entered **monthly** rate, rounded to cents. One fixed month-end payment; final payment is capped. No new charges, fees or rate changes. Payments at/below interest and payoff beyond 1,200 months are rejected. Real cards can use daily-balance methods, so lender statements will differ.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Compound growth    | `initial*(1+r)^n + monthly*((1+r)^n-1)/r`, month-end contributions; zero-rate case is initial + contributions. `r` is assumed nominal annual growth ÷ 1,200. No intermediate cent rounding. Fixed nonnegative returns do not model market volatility or losses; taxes, fees and inflation are excluded.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Savings goal       | Solve the compound-growth formula for the monthly contribution; round **up** to cents so the illustrated target is met. Set growth to 0% for a contribution-only plan. Already sufficient savings require zero added monthly contributions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Time deposit       | Simple gross interest = principal × annual rate × actual days / 36,500. Round gross interest to cents, then calculate/round entered withholding on that gross interest. Maturity = principal + gross − withholding. Actual/365, no compounding or early withdrawal. Tax is user-provided; other provider conventions differ.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Emergency fund     | Target = monthly essentials × chosen months of coverage. Gap = max(0, target − current fund). Months = ceiling(gap / monthly contribution); zero contribution with a gap has no estimated completion. No growth or withdrawals.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

Loan and card schedules can be reviewed and exported to CSV. Export uses numeric values only and never includes account names, bank details or credentials. All tool-specific assumptions are also visible in the app. These estimates should be compared with an actual lender/deposit statement or offer before making a commitment.
